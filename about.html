Who We Are

Our Story

PLUTEK (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology company founded by passionate third-year university students with a vision of creating innovative, affordable and scalable digital solutions for businesses across Africa. We believe technology should solve real business problems while remaining accessible to organisations of every size.

To empower businesses through modern software, cloud technologies, automation and strategic IT consulting that improves productivity and drives sustainable growth.
