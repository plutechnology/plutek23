PluTek is a South African technology company founded in 2026. We help businesses use technology more effectively, starting with a website that works and growing from there.

Why we started

Too many South African businesses are paying for websites that look dated on a phone, load slowly and never bring in an enquiry, or for software that doesn't fit how they work.

We started PluTek to do that work properly: design that builds trust, code that's fast and secure, and straight answers about what's worth spending money on. We'd rather build one thing that works than sell three that don't.
