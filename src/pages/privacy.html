Privacy notice

How we handle the personal information you send us through this website. Last updated 8 November 2026.

PluTek (Pty) Ltd (“PluTek”, “we”) respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Contact and project forms: your name, email, phone number, company and the details you give us about your project, including any file you attach.
  • Talent network form: your name, contact details, area of work, links you share, your CV and any note you add.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare and deliver the work you ask for, and, for the talent network, to consider you for roles at PluTek. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions and files are stored by our website host, Netlify, Inc., which may process data outside South Africa under contractual safeguards. Messages you send on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp. Only the PluTek founders have access to submissions.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and to manage any work that follows. Talent network applications are deleted after 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
