How a project runs

The same four stages for a five-page website or a custom system. You always know what happens next.

  1. Consultation

    A conversation about your business, your customers and what the project has to achieve.

  2. Planning

    A clear scope, page structure and timeline, agreed before any design or code begins.

  3. Design and build

    We design, develop and test on real phones and browsers, sharing progress as we go.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, check everything works in production, and stay on for maintenance and upgrades.

Questions we hear most

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How long does a website take?

Usually between one and four weeks, depending on how many pages and features it needs. You'll get a timeline with your scope before work starts.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We rebuild existing sites to look current, load faster and work properly on phones, and we keep the content and links that are already working for you.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. Booking systems, internal tools and dashboards can be shaped around how your business already works, instead of forcing you into off-the-shelf software.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer hosting, maintenance and technical support so your site stays secure, backed up and up to date.
